## Title

AstroVital: Visual Astronaut Wellness Tracker | Space Apps 2026

## Description

AstroVital is an installable web app and Android app that shows an astronaut's wellness status before, during and after a mission. A 3D body model highlights attention zones (red) and stable indicators (green), and the app reads live solar flare data. Educational project, not a medical device.
